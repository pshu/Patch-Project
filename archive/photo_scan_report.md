## Source discrepancies and unverified items

- GPS III-8: confirmed by the user as SV10 (Apr 21, 2026, B1095 flight 7). GPS III-7 confirmed as May 30, 2025 (SLC-40).
- LIGHTSAIL-2: center core serial B1057 and the side-booster landing assignment come from a single source or recollection.
- T1TL-E: booster B1103 flight 4 versus 5 depending on source. Space.com calls the mission "T1TL-3".
- STARSHIP-F13: the patch is sold as Flight 13. Booster B20 flight 1 is assumed. Landing outcomes rest on Wikipedia only.
- MRV-1: B1069 flight 32 (one source says "31+"). Landing site is not applicable, the booster was expended.
- VIASAT-3-F3: booster serials and landing zones come from Spaceflight Now only. LZ-40 is not independently confirmed.
- SPCX-LIFTOFF-2026: commemorative patch, no flight. Dates come from Wikipedia.
- CRS-32 and Dragon splashdown dates: single source (Wikipedia).
- Not added (unidentified, no mission number on patch): Starlink gold hexagon (IMG_1254), Starlink round Falcon 9 (IMG_1256).
